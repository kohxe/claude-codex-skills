At most two distinct visual effects are added, and they are confined to the hero section. Every noticeable decorative layer counts as its own effect (grain/noise, glow, decorative or animated overlay, particles, text animation); grain that is part of a grainy gradient counts once together with it, and a static scrim or panel that exists only for text contrast does not count.
